> so currently, what is actually being saved in persistance? both the ai
analysis and the audio transcriptions? or just the audio transcriptions or
just the ai-analysis?

● Both are saved - the system persists audio transcriptions (what was said
  by each speaker) in the transcripts table AND AI analysis (strategic
  insights categorized as opportunities, cautions, risks, next-steps) in the
   insights table, along with meeting metadata.


> on a single line, isnt now overkill to have localStorage saving?

● No - localStorage provides instant read/write during active meetings
  (0-latency) and offline capability, while SQLite ensures durability; it's
  a deliberate performance/offline-first caching strategy, not overkill.
